Sigiriya Rock Fortress

★★★★★ 4.9 (2,140 reviews) · Matale District, Central Province

About this place

Rising 200 metres above the jungle, Sigiriya is a 5th-century royal citadel built by King Kashyapa. Climb past the famous frescoes and the Lion's Paw terrace to panoramic views over the Cultural Triangle. Allow 3–4 hours; early morning visits avoid heat and crowds.
