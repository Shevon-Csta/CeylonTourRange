How do fare estimates work for taxi bookings?
Fares are fixed at booking time based on distance, vehicle type, and season. What you see at confirmation is what you pay — tolls and A/C included. Waiting time beyond 30 minutes is billed at LKR 500/hour.
Can I cancel or change a hotel booking?
Most rooms include free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. The exact policy is shown on the room card before payment and in your dashboard under Bookings.
How do I share an itinerary with my travel group?
Open your itinerary, press Share, and send the link. Anyone with the link can view; people you invite by email can edit days, add stops, and comment.
How do I become a verified tour guide?
Apply from the Guide Portal with your SLTDA licence number. Verification takes 2–3 working days, after which you can list places, hotels, and events.
Which currencies and payment methods are supported?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and PayPal, and display prices in USD, EUR, GBP, and LKR. Taxi rides can also be paid in cash.
